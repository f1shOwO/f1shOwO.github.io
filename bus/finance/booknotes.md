# Remember: Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity

### Vocabulary

- Liability: Legal amount of money owed to another party.
- Owner's Equity: Portion of a company that the owner has control over.
- Assets: Anything of value that is owned or controlled by an individual.
- Original Investment: Total amount of money placed into a company at its beginning.
- Notes Payable: A legal contract where a borrower agrees to pay back a lender the money plus intrest after some time.
- Inventory: A list of products and goods that a company has for ownership, sale or use. 
- Sales: An exchange of a product or service for goods or money.
- Costs of Goods Sold: Total cost of producing or purchasing the products of a company during a certain time.
- Expenses: Cost of operations, goods, services, or personal needs that requires effort, time, or money. 
- Balance Sheet: A financial statement that provides imformation regarding a company's position at a point in time.
